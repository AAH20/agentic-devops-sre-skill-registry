# Economics contract

```text
served requests      = min(demand, candidate capacity)
successful outcomes  = served requests × success rate
cost per success     = total candidate cost ÷ successful outcomes
revenue              = successful outcomes × value per success
contribution margin  = revenue − included model/infrastructure cost
```

Record the window, currency, demand, tokens per request, value per success, capacity/cost, success rate, P95 latency, quality, availability, residency, GPU utilization, and excluded costs. Contribution margin is not accounting gross profit unless every delivery cost is included.
